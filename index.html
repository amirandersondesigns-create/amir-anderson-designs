Work

I turn ideas into things people see, feel, and remember.

Amir Anderson is a two-time Emmy Award–winning production designer whose work spans graphic design, motion design, broadcast graphics, branding, and scenic design. A Full Sail University graduate, he has worked across news, sports, and entertainment with some of the largest media companies.

He started his career at WarnerMedia, creating social and motion content for Bleacher Report, NBA TV, and NBA on TNT. From there, he moved into broadcast design and production with Warner Bros. Discovery, working with HLN and CNN on on-air graphics, motion packages, branding, scenic design, and visual storytelling.

Amir also enjoys building the systems behind the work. From templates and workflows to custom plugins and tools, he looks for ways to help creative teams work faster, stay consistent, and spend more time focusing on the ideas themselves. To him, the tooling and process are just as much a part of design as the final product.

He is always learning, exploring new ways of working, and trying to better understand the creative processes behind great work. He considers himself a student of design—always looking for something new to learn, build, and improve.
